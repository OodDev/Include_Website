  title: "From training to employment: closing the gap"
  lead: "INCLUDE – From protected paths to real jobs addresses a persistent challenge in inclusive vocational education and training: the transition from learning and protected experiences to meaningful participation in real workplaces."
closing:
  paragraphs:
    - Young adults with cognitive disabilities can develop valuable vocational competences, but these competences do not always become visible, transferable or connected to actual labour-market opportunities. At the same time, SMEs may be open to inclusion while lacking the organisational tools, mentoring capacity and practical guidance needed to host and support inclusive work-based learning.
    - INCLUDE works on both sides of this transition. It supports learners in progressively developing and demonstrating competences, while helping companies understand their own readiness, adapt workflows and create supportive workplace environments.
approach:
  title: A corporate-led approach
  paragraphs:
    - Companies are not treated simply as final hosts for internships. They are involved as active co-designers of vocational pathways. Real company workflows and labour-market needs inform the vocational profiles, the learning experiences and the competences to be recognised.
    - The project combines research, participatory dialogue, competence-based vocational design, mentoring, workplace adaptation and competence recognition to build a model that can be tested in real contexts and transferred across Europe.
