statement:
  title: Why companies?
  body: Companies are where vocational inclusion ultimately becomes real.\n\nToo often, young adults with cognitive disabilities develop skills through training and protected work experiences, but the transition to real employment remains difficult. At the same time, many companies are willing to become more inclusive but lack practical tools, adapted workflows and structured support to turn this intention into sustainable opportunities.\n\nINCLUDE brings these two worlds together, supporting both people in their transition to employment and companies in becoming workplaces ready for inclusion.
  pull: Internships may work, but they do not always become jobs. INCLUDE works on the person, and together with the company, because inclusion is the workplace that learns to include. # needsClientApproval

  - key: research
    titleKey: home.ambitions.research.title
    body: We will explore the conditions that make inclusive employment possible, bringing together the perspectives of companies, VET providers, people with disabilities and local stakeholders. Through labour market analysis, interviews and participatory activities, we will investigate company needs, barriers and opportunities and develop a Corporate Inclusion Readiness Index.
    icon: data-impact
  - key: pathway
    titleKey: home.ambitions.pathway.title
    body: We will co-design and test a vocational pathway supporting young adults with cognitive disabilities in the transition from protected learning environments to real workplaces. The model will connect Protected Gyms, pre-corporate experiences and work-based learning in SMEs, supported by vocational profiles, mentoring and tools to recognise and make competences visible.
    icon: pathway
  - key: network
    titleKey: home.ambitions.network.title
    body: We will support companies in becoming active players in inclusive employment. Practical tools, a Ready for Inclusion framework and label, and a European network of Ambassador SMEs will help companies start, strengthen and share inclusive work-based learning practices.
