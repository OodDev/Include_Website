## What personal data this website processes and why

The INCLUDE website is designed so that no personal data is collected while you browse: no cookies or other storage beyond strictly necessary first-party storage, no third-party requests. When you send a **contact form request**, name, email address, organisation, country and any message you write are used **only** to reply, are delivered to the project mailbox, are not stored in a database and are not used for marketing. Their processing is based on your consent given via the acknowledgement checkbox in the form. If you subscribe to the **newsletter**, we process your email address with your explicit consent, to send project updates; subscription uses double opt-in and you can unsubscribe with one click at any time.

## Recipients, retention, transfers

Contact messages are sent to the project mailbox and kept only as long as needed to handle the request; newsletter data is processed by the newsletter provider acting under a data-processing agreement.
