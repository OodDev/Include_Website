## What this website stores

The INCLUDE website is built with data minimisation by design (§11.1): while you browse, it sets **no non-essential cookies or other storage** of any kind, and makes **no third-party requests**. Only strictly necessary first-party storage exists.

## Storage inventory

| Name | Purpose | Category | Duration | Provider |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `include-consent` | Stores your privacy choices (categories, timestamp, policy version) so the site can respect them and honour withdrawal | Strictly necessary | ≤ 6 months, or until you change them / withdraw | INCLUDE website (first-party) **TODO(owner): confirm with legal** |

If third-party statistics or embedded media are enabled in a future version, they will load **only** after your consent, and every item will be listed here.

## Changing your choices

Use the **"Cookie settings"** link in the footer at any time to review or withdraw your choices. Withdrawal works immediately and does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.
